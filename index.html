Design system

The tokens and components this site is built from, in whichever theme you are viewing.

Color

  • Background--bg
  • Surface--surface
  • Ink--ink
  • Text--text
  • Accent--accent
  • Accent text--accent-text

The accent is for small things: links, focus, selected states. Ratios are against the background.

Type

Figtree 600–700, headings

Inter 400–600, body at 18px / 1.6

Spacing

  • 8
  • 16
  • 24
  • 32
  • 48
  • 64

Components and states

Primary Secondary Focused Tag
  • 3px focus ring on everything you can reach by keyboard
  • Touch targets at least 44px
  • Text contrast at least 4.5:1 in both themes
  • Motion off when you ask for reduced motion
